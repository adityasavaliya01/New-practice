# Mall Customer Segmentation using Unsupervised Learning

## Project Overview

This project performs customer segmentation on the Mall Customers dataset using three unsupervised machine learning algorithms:

- K-Means Clustering
- Agglomerative Hierarchical Clustering
- DBSCAN Clustering

The objective is to identify groups of customers with similar shopping behaviour based on demographic and spending information. These customer segments help businesses design targeted marketing strategies, improve customer engagement, and increase sales.

---

## Dataset

**Dataset Name:** Mall Customers Dataset

**Features**

- CustomerID
- Gender
- Age
- Annual Income (k$)
- Spending Score (1–100)

## Shopper Persona Descriptions

### Big Spenders

Customers with high annual income and high spending scores. They frequently purchase premium products and respond well to exclusive offers and VIP services.

### Young Aspirers

Young customers with relatively low income but high spending behaviour. They are attracted by fashion, entertainment, and promotional campaigns.

### Average Customers

Customers with moderate income and average spending habits. Loyalty programs and personalized recommendations help retain these customers.

### Budget Shoppers

Customers with low income and low spending scores. They are highly price-sensitive and prefer discounts, cashback offers, and value-for-money products.

### Mature Savers

Customers with high income but relatively low spending scores. Premium experiences and personalized promotions can encourage higher spending.
